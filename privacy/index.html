Privacy Policy

PingPal: Table Tennis · Last updated June 3, 2026

No data collected

PingPal is a table-tennis scorekeeping app. Your privacy is simple because the app is built to keep everything on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. PingPal does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in the app — matches, players, scores, and statistics — is stored locally on your device. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Tracking & third parties

PingPal contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs. There is nothing to opt out of.

Deleting your data

Because all data is stored on-device, deleting the app removes all of its data. You can also delete individual players or matches inside the app.

Children

PingPal is safe for all ages: it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Reach out via GitHub Issues.

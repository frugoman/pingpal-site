PingPal: Table Tennis

Thanks for using PingPal — the quick way to keep score in table tennis. Set your phone on the table, tap a side to score, and PingPal handles the rules.

Quick tips

  • Score a point: tap the side that won the rally.
  • Made a mistake? Tap Undo — it reverses the last point.
  • Serving: the serving side is highlighted; in doubles, PingPal shows who serves and who receives.
  • Deuce, games & ends: win-by-two, best-of-N, and switching ends are all handled automatically.
  • Rematch: when a match ends, tap Rematch to play the same lineup again.
  • History & stats: completed matches are saved on your device with per-player win/loss.
